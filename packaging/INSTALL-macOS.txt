Tosc - macOS (Apple silicon + Intel, macOS 11 or newer)
An Okllo instrument - https://okllo.com - https://github.com/inlxs/tosc

Plug-ins
  AU   (Logic, GarageBand):  copy "Tosc.component" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  VST3 (Ableton, Bitwig ...): copy "Tosc.vst3"      to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  CLAP (Bitwig, Reaper ...): copy "Tosc.clap"      to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP
Standalone
  Copy "Tosc.app" to /Applications.

These builds are not notarised by Apple yet. After copying, remove the download quarantine once
in Terminal, otherwise macOS says the plug-in "is damaged" or "cannot be opened":

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Tosc.component"
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Tosc.vst3"
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/"Tosc.clap"
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/"Tosc.app"

Then rescan plug-ins in your DAW (in Logic: restart it). It appears under the maker "Okllo".
An internet connection is needed to load maps and elevation; playing saved tables works offline.
